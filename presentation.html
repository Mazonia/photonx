SLIDE 02 / 07 • THE PROBLEM ASTRONOMICAL BOTTLENECK

The Problem: The Hidden Giants of the Outer Solar System

Searching for hypothetical Planet X and cold brown dwarfs across millions of celestial infrared sources.

❄️ Invisible at Optical

Beyond Neptune, cold objects (~250K) reflect virtually zero visible sunlight. They can only be observed through thermal near-infrared emission.

📊 The Data Tsunami

NASA's SPHEREx will image the entire sky in 102 distinct infrared bands every 6 months, yielding billions of complex multi-extension FITS data points.

🧱 Accessibility Barrier

Raw astronomical data requires specialized command-line tools (IRAF, DS9) and deep domain knowledge, preventing public discovery participation.

🎯 The Core Opportunity

A cold outer body moving against stationary background stars produces a unmistakable temporal positional shift across 6-month survey cadences.

SLIDE 05 / 07 • SCIENTIFIC VALIDITY NASA DATA INTEGRATION

Scientific Validity & Multi-Mission NASA Data

Deep integration with primary NASA mission datasets, automated pixel differencing, and live Open APIs.

🌈 102-Band Spectra

Extracts complete spectrophotometric curves (0.75–5.00 µm) with diagnostic markers for biogenic ices (H₂O, CO, CO₂) and interstellar organic PAH emissions.

📉 Pixel Differencing

Computes image difference residuals (Epoch B − Epoch A). Connected-component clustering isolates significant >3σ flux anomalies and proper-motion vectors.

🚀 Live NASA APIs

Direct integration with NASA APOD, NeoWs near-Earth asteroid tracking (miss distances in LD/km), DONKI space weather (CMEs/GSTs), and DSCOVR EPIC Earth imagery.

🛰️ Official Provenance & Verification

Primary Data: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA) SPHEREx QR2 • DOI: 10.26131/IRSA629
